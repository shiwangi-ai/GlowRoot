Arrange only the actual recipe produce around the bottle base, completely within the frame. Small tasteful botanical fruit illustration printed above the label. Printed dark green serif brand text exactly "GlowRoot", small subtitle exactly "Nature's Palette", then product name exactly as specified. No other text, no medical claims, no watermark, no sparkles, no collage or additional bottles. Crisp readable label, realistic fruit texture.
